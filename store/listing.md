**App name:** Easy Quran

**Short description (80 chars max):**
One ayah at a time with Urdu translation, namaz times, Qibla and tracker.

**Full description:**
Easy Quran helps you read the whole Quran slowly, one ayah at a time, in order from the first ayah onward.

• Full Quran reader: all 114 surahs and 30 juz, Arabic with Urdu translation by Maulana Syed Abul A'la Maududi
• Resume reading where you stopped, adjustable text size, share and copy ayat
• Optional: your wallpaper moves to the next ayah, with a new mosque scene, every time you unlock your phone
• Text colour adjusts to the background so it stays readable
• Daily ayah reminder
• Namaz times for Pakistani cities, calculated on your phone (no internet needed)
• Prayer alarms, a daily namaz tracker with 7-day history and streak
• Qibla compass, Hijri date and tasbeeh counter
• Search by word, surah name or reference such as 2:255
• Bookmarks and personal notes
• Audio recitation (internet needed)
• Add your own background photos
• No ads, no accounts, no data collection

Prayer times are calculated using the University of Islamic Sciences, Karachi method (Hanafi Asr) and may differ from your local mosque by a few minutes. Please follow your mosque's announced times.
