Easy Quran – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Easy Quran does not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics.

Data kept on your phone

Your reading position, bookmarks, notes, chosen city, prayer alarm settings, prayer tracker history, tasbeeh count and any photos or translation files you import are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app removes them.

Location

The app does not use GPS. You choose a Pakistani city from a list, and prayer times are calculated on your phone.

Network use

If you press the audio button, your phone requests a recitation file from everyayah.com, which can see your IP address as any website would. No other network requests are made.

Permissions

Notifications (ayah and prayer reminders), alarms (prayer time alerts), wallpaper (changing your wallpaper at your request), foreground service (running the unlock feature you turn on), and internet (audio only).

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects nothing from anyone.
